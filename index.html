01 / About us

3D printing should be for everyone.

3D for Everyone (3D4E) is a student organization at USC that helps beginners learn 3D printing while giving experienced makers room to keep building. Members can learn printer operation, develop CAD and modeling skills, and get support turning ideas into physical designs.

02 / What we do

Start with a workshop.
Stay for the making.

01

Weekly workshops

Learn practical skills in CAD, 3D modeling, slicing, printer operation, additive manufacturing, and design for manufacturing.

Open to everyone. Attendance is not required for membership.

02

Optional lab access

Print your own designs in our dedicated lab with 10+ printers, filament, and help from experienced lab technicians.

Explore different materials, sizes, multicolor printing, modification, and troubleshooting.

03

Annual showcase

See what members and fellow 3D4E chapters have made, meet other makers, and get inspired for your next idea.

Visitors and friends are welcome—you do not need a finished project to attend.

10+ 3D printers available

03 / The lab

Room to experiment.

Members can work with printers including the Prusa CORE One, ELEGOO Neptune Max, and Bambu Lab P1S. Lab activities are completely optional and are not required for membership.

  • Unlimited printing and filament
  • Guidance from lab technicians
  • Printer building and troubleshooting
  • Different materials and print sizes
